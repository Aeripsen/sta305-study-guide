How it is put together

Questions are the spine. Each one is followed by:

  • What this question is really asking — what he is actually testing, where the trap is, and what shape the answer takes, before any algebra.
  • The full solution, one step per line, with a why note under each line explaining where that number came from.

What is inside

  • Reference card: every df formula and everything not on the provided formula sheet
  • Part 0: what each symbol means and why ANOVA exists at all
  • Four complete past papers, worked end to end
  • Extra drills for the units those papers never touched
  • 17 question variants and 20 multiple choice
  • A predicted 8-question paper to sit under timed conditions
Questions are quoted from posted past papers and practice problems for private study. All arithmetic was re-derived and checked against the posted answer keys. No original course files (lecture slides, practice PDFs, solutions, exam papers) are included in this repository.
